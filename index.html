The Orbit Spectrum

Where are you right now?

Antigravity maps four distinct states of being — from complete mechanical absorption to conscious, balanced selfhood. Every person exists somewhere on this spectrum at any given moment. Awareness of your position is the first act of antigravity.

0 — Free Fall 26 — Falling 51 — Approaching 76 — In Orbit — 100
🌍

Score 0 – 25

Free Fall

Fully absorbed in mechanical, automatic patterns. The environment is running you. Habits are indistinguishable from identity.

↓

Score 26 – 50

Falling

Gravitational pull is dominant. Some moments of awareness — but they don't yet interrupt the patterns. Drifting toward earth.

↑

Score 51 – 75

Approaching

Awareness is growing. You can observe patterns before or during them. Antigravity practice has taken root. Orbit is within reach.

🌙

Score 76 – 100

In Orbit

Neither falling nor drifting. Authentic, conscious, balanced. You respond rather than react. This is the stable orbit of your true self.
